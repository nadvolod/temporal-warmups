PRE-TEMPORAL Wellness Purchase — Broken Process

Step through the process. Watch what happens to system state when payment fails.

Step 0 of 5
⚡ Process Flow
1
Submit Intake Form
Patient submits product + dosage info. Creates an intake record in the DB.
2
Wait for Provider Approval
A healthcare provider must review the request. In this version: simulated with a 1-second wait. No real signal mechanism exists.
3
Record Approval
Approval is stored in the DB. Now the intake + approval records both exist.
4
Process Payment
Charges the patient's card.
5
Send Prescription
Delivers the prescription to the patient. Only reached if payment succeeded.

⚠️ Orphaned state — nothing cleaned up
  • The intake form is still "active" in the database
  • The approval was never revoked — the provider thinks it's still valid
  • The patient may believe they have an approved prescription
  • No retry will run automatically — the process is just stuck

This is what Temporal + the Saga pattern solves: compensation activities undo each step in reverse order when something fails.

Legend
● Active — record exists and is valid ● Orphaned — stuck, nothing will clean it up ● Empty — not created yet
